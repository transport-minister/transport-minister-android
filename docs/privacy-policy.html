Transport Minister – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Transport Minister is a city transport game for Android published by Jahangir Alisoy ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses.

Information the game stores

Your game progress (cities, money, diamonds, achievements, settings) is stored only on your device. We do not run any server and we do not receive, collect or store your personal data.

Advertising

The game shows optional rewarded videos through Google AdMob. Ads are only shown when you choose to watch one. Google may collect the advertising ID of your device and other information to show and measure ads, as described in Google's advertising policy. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before showing ads, and you can change your choice at any time from the consent form.

Purchases

Diamond packs are sold through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment; we never see your payment details. Purchased diamonds are stored on your device.

Children

The game is suitable for everyone and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game removes all game data from your device.
